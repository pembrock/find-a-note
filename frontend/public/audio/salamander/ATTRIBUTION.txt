Find a Note bundles the 30 MP3 files from the Tone.js distribution unchanged.
This is a reduced, single dynamic layer selection of the original library,
not the complete multi-layer Salamander sound bank. Intermediate notes are
repitched at playback; velocity scales amplitude. No endorsement is implied.
